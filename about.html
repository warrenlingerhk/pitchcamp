About PitchCamp

PitchCamp.live is the online home of Certified Innovation Consulting and Training, founded by Dr. Warren Linger. We help founders and entrepreneurs master the art of the pitch — so they can raise capital, win customers, and lead with confidence.

Our Mission

To turn nervous presenters into compelling storytellers. We believe pitching is about relationships first, and that when you connect with your audience authentically, everything else follows.

What We Do

PitchCamp runs intensive, practice-driven programs covering storytelling, frame control, status building, Q&A mastery, and impact strategies. You don't just learn — you pitch, get feedback, and improve in a supportive community of fellow founders.

Our Approach

Small groups. Real practice. Honest, structured feedback (Like Best / Even Better If). And frameworks you can reuse for the rest of your founding journey. We keep it fun, because learning sticks when you're enjoying it.
